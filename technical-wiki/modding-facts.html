World coordinates and object lifecycle 7 facts

Where objects are, when the game switches them off, and when it destroys them.

  1. 1

    Fact 1: Floating origin at 1,000 m

    Game-specific#

    NWH.Common.FloatingOrigin sits on the Player with a distance threshold of 1,000 m. It exposes TotalOffset, the sum of all shifts so far.

    Related: 2 What a shift moves, 3 Store positions relative to MapMagicSource notes: cavedarkness-notes, despawn-research, apocaplayer-2.3.2

  2. 2

    Fact 2: What a shift moves

    Game-specific#

    When the main camera gets 1,000 m from the current origin, FloatingOrigin's LateUpdate translates every root transform in all loaded scenes by the negative camera position. Relative positions are unchanged; absolute world coordinates jump.

    In practiceChildren of a root, and objects you create as scene roots, move with the world automatically. NPCs, and objects deactivated far away, are roots and are shifted too. Any position cached from an earlier frame (camera, eye, target, bounds) is wrong on the shift frame.

    Related: 3 Store positions relative to MapMagicSource notes: cavedarkness-notes, apocaplayer-2.3.2

  3. 3

    Fact 3: Store positions relative to MapMagic

    Game-specific#

    Absolute Unity world coordinates are not stable across shifts. The MapMagic root object is a stable anchor: positions stored relative to it survive any number of shifts.

    In practiceStore long-lived positions as offsets from the MapMagic anchor and add Anchor.position on every read. Multi-frame jobs (bakes, paths) must re-read the anchor each step.

    Related: 2 What a shift movesSource notes: cavedarkness-notes, apocalypterai-compat

  4. 4

    Fact 4: Registered items switch off beyond 1 km

    Game-specificCorrected#

    __GameManager__ :: DeactivateFarAwayItems checks ArrayList_Items every 5 s. Objects more than 1,000 m from the Player are set inactive and are reactivated when the Player comes back within range. Objects on layer 22 (Map) are exempt, and cars are not in this list.

    In practiceA deactivated object no longer receives SaveGame and its FSMs restart when it is reactivated.

    Related: 13 Inactive objects miss SaveGame, 26 FSMs restart on re-enable, 6 Cars never deactivate, go kinematic beyond 2 kmSource notes: despawn-research

    Revised 2026-10-13. Corrected: names the list, adds reactivation and the layer 22 exemption.

  5. 5

    Fact 5: Items and cars are destroyed beyond 5 km

    Game-specificCorrected#

    DeleteFarAwayItems runs every 10 s and destroys registered items and cars more than 5,000 m from the Player, including all their children: attached parts, cargo locked to a bed, seated NPCs. Sandstorms (ArrayList_Sandstorms) are destroyed beyond 50,000 m, and anything that falls below y = -10,000 is destroyed by DeleteFallingItems.

    In practiceAnything a mod parents under a car or item is destroyed with it. Caches that hold references to registered objects must tolerate destroyed entries.

    Related: 6 Cars never deactivate, go kinematic beyond 2 kmSource notes: despawn-research

    Revised 2026-10-13. Corrected: states explicitly that cars are destroyed too.

  6. 6

    Fact 6: Cars never deactivate, go kinematic beyond 2 km

    Game-specificCorrected#

    Cars are not deactivated by distance. Each car's DistanceKinematic FSM makes its Rigidbody kinematic beyond 2,000 m and non-kinematic again inside it. Cars are still destroyed beyond 5,000 m. Between 1 and 2 km a car is active and physical while its registered loose parts and cargo are already deactivated.

    In practiceDo not toggle DistanceKinematic on a moving car: re-enabling it restarts it in KinematicOn, which zeroes the car's velocity.

    Related: 5 Items and cars are destroyed beyond 5 km, 26 FSMs restart on re-enableSource notes: despawn-research, apocapatrol-notes

    Revised 2026-10-13. Corrected: cars are exempt only from deactivation, not from destruction.

  7. 7

    Fact 7: Distances are measured from the Player

    Game-specific#

    The deactivation, destruction and kinematic checks use the distance from the Player object. While driving, the Player is parented inside the car, so the distances follow the car. The floating origin, by contrast, follows the main camera.

    Related: 48 The Player rides inside the carSource notes: despawn-research

Registration and persistence 9 facts

What the game saves and culls, and how a spawned object joins those systems.

  1. 8

    Fact 8: The three registries

    Game-specific#

    NewGO_ArrayList holds three PlayMakerArrayListProxy lists: ArrayList_Items (items, weapons, loose parts, NPCs, corpses), ArrayList_Cars (vehicles and trailers) and ArrayList_Sandstorms.

    Related: 9 Registration decides saving and cullingSource notes: despawn-research, apocasaver-notes

  2. 9

    Fact 9: Registration decides saving and culling

    Game-specific#

    Only objects in these lists are saved and distance-culled. Unregistered objects, such as sanity hallucination mobs or mod spawns that were never added, are never saved and never despawned.

    Related: 12 Save-compatible spawn recipeSource notes: despawn-research, apocaspawner-notes

  3. 10

    Fact 10: Clone names are stable identities

    Game-specific#

    Spawners name clones <prefab>(Clone)N, where N comes from the global itemNameID counter. The name is unique and survives save and load, so it can be used as a persistent identity for mod data.

    Related: 12 Save-compatible spawn recipeSource notes: apocapocket-rebuild-spec, apocaraiders-notes

  4. 11

    Fact 11: Randomize initialises spawned objects

    Game-specific#

    Spawners send the Randomize event to a new object; item FSMs use it to roll contents such as ammo counts (6 to 12 rounds in an ammo box).

    In practiceDisable the object's Randomize FSM if a mod needs to set those values itself.

    Related: 12 Save-compatible spawn recipeSource notes: apocaunloader-notes, trailers-research

  5. 12

    Fact 12: Save-compatible spawn recipe

    Game-specificAdded#

    The vanilla spawn sequence is: instantiate, increment itemNameID, rename to <prefab>(Clone)N, add to the right list once, optionally send Randomize. Adding the same object to a list twice duplicates it on the next load.

    In practiceRemove a mod object by dropping it and all its descendants from every list, then destroying it.

    Related: 10 Clone names are stable identities, 11 Randomize initialises spawned objectsSource notes: apocaspawner-notes, apogarage-spawn-research, apocapatrol-notes

    Revised 2026-10-13. Added.

  6. 13

    Fact 13: Inactive objects miss SaveGame

    Game-specific#

    Inactive GameObjects do not receive the game's SaveGame event, so their FSM state is not written.

    In practiceHide objects that must still be saved by disabling renderers and colliders, not with SetActive(false).

    Related: 4 Registered items switch off beyond 1 kmSource notes: item-slots-research

  7. 14

    Fact 14: ES3 saves components on saved roots

    Game-specific#

    ES3 serialises the components on the root of each saved object, including components a mod added at runtime. Children such as an item's Light are not saved.

    In practiceKeep mod state in sidecar files rather than components on saved objects; a save carrying mod components can fail to load without the mod.

    Related: 15 Only referenced car children are savedSource notes: apocaraiders-notes, partadjustment-notes

  8. 15

    Fact 15: Only referenced car children are saved

    Game-specific#

    A car is saved as its root object plus the children its own FSMs reference (for example Fuel, hinge_exhaust). Children a mod adds at runtime are not saved.

    Related: 14 ES3 saves components on saved rootsSource notes: buggy-body-research, item-slots-research

  9. 16

    Fact 16: The world reappears over several seconds

    Game-specificCorrected#

    After a load, saved NPCs, items and a worn backpack are restored progressively over several seconds. In the same period MapMagic regenerates terrain and points of interest (camps, buildings, caves) from the world seed; points of interest are not stored in the save.

    In practiceUse grace periods and retries after a load (NPCAI waits up to 20 s in 0.5 s steps) and never cache a 'not found' result permanently.

    Related: 22 SaveLoadGame state sequenceSource notes: apocapocket-2.0-notes, npcai-review-1.0.1, cavedarkness-notes

    Revised 2026-10-13. Corrected: points of interest are regenerated, not loaded from the save.

Save system 8 facts

How Easy Save 3 is driven by the SaveLoadGame FSM, and when mod data can safely be written.

  1. 17

    Fact 17: Easy Save 3, plain JSON slots

    Game-specific#

    Saves use Easy Save 3. Slots are SaveGameN.es3 (plain JSON) in %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\SawyerK Games\Apocalypter\; slot timestamps are in SaveSettings.es3.

    Related: 18 Saves go through an in-memory cacheSource notes: apocasaver-notes

  2. 18

    Fact 18: Saves go through an in-memory cache

    Game-specific#

    ES3's default location is Cache: the game writes the whole save into memory, and the cache is flushed to the slot file at the end of the save sequence. Changes written directly to the file before that flush are overwritten.

    Related: 23 When mod data can be writtenSource notes: apocasaver-notes, apocapocket-rebuild-spec

  3. 19

    Fact 19: The active slot

    Game-specific#

    ES3Settings.defaultSettings.path identifies the active save slot during normal play. The slot buttons set it through ES3SettingsMod.SetSavePath.

    Related: 20 The slot path is unreliable during a load, 21 FSM SaveFile variables are staleSource notes: apocasaver-notes, apocapocket-2.0-notes

  4. 20

    Fact 20: The slot path is unreliable during a load

    Game-specific#

    While a save is loading, the active path can be empty or still point to the previous slot.

    In practiceRecord the slot when saving and check it again after the load completes.

    Related: 19 The active slotSource notes: apocasaver-notes, apocapocket-2.0-notes

  5. 21

    Fact 21: FSM SaveFile variables are stale

    Game-specific#

    FSM variables such as SaveFile on SaveLoadGame and NewGO_ArrayList are only updated on load and can point to another slot (one showed SaveGame1 while slot 10 was loading).

    Related: 19 The active slotSource notes: apocasaver-notes

  6. 22

    Fact 22: SaveLoadGame state sequence

    Game-specific#

    Save: SaveGame event, wait, SaveGame, back to isPlay. Load: LoadGame, generateTerrain 2, LoadVar, loaded, terrain wait, isPlay. New game: Start, setSeed, generateTerrain, isPlay. A load from the main menu can skip Start.

    In practiceReset per-game mod state on Start, setSeed and LoadGame. Restore mod state about 0.5 s after isPlay following a load.

    Related: 23 When mod data can be writtenSource notes: apocasaver-notes, apocapocket-2.0-notes

  7. 23

    Fact 23: When mod data can be written

    Game-specific#

    Save_NewGO_ArrayList flushes the cache in its state store cached file. Mod data is safe if it is written into the cache before that flush, or to the file (and cache) about 1 s after SaveLoadGame returns to isPlay.

    Related: 18 Saves go through an in-memory cacheSource notes: apocasaver-notes, apocapocket-rebuild-spec

  8. 24

    Fact 24: ES3.Load overload trap

    Game-specificAdded#

    ES3.Load<string>(key, "", settings) binds to the overload (key, filePath, settings) and reads a file named "". Use ES3.Load<T>(key, settings).

    Source notes: apocasaver-notes, apocapocket-rebuild-spec

    Revised 2026-10-13. Added.

PlayMaker, input and game state 10 facts

Behaviour of the FSMs that run almost all gameplay logic.

  1. 25

    Fact 25: Gameplay is PlayMaker

    Game-specific#

    Almost all gameplay logic is PlayMaker FSMs: 1,357 of the 1,947 types in Assembly-CSharp are PlayMaker actions. The remaining C# is asset-store code (Easy Save 3, NWH Vehicle Physics 2, MapMagic, sky, input and sensor libraries).

    In practiceMods read the FSMs, then drive them with SendEvent, Fsm.SetState, FSM variables, or Harmony patches on PlayMaker action classes.

    Source notes: PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS

  2. 26

    Fact 26: FSMs restart on re-enable

    PlayMaker#

    Fsm.RestartOnEnable defaults to true, so any FSM whose GameObject or component is re-enabled starts again from its start state.

    In practiceSet RestartOnEnable = false before toggling an object you need to resume in place, and restore it afterwards.

    Related: 27 Third-person camera restarts PlayerCamera FSMs, 28 CheckTag detaches parts on restartSource notes: PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS, apocapatrol-notes

  3. 27

    Fact 27: Third-person camera restarts PlayerCamera FSMs

    Game-specific#

    Switching to the third-person car camera disables the whole PlayerCamera hierarchy, so every FSM under it restarts when the camera comes back.

    Related: 28 CheckTag detaches parts on restartSource notes: apocapocket-rebuild-spec

  4. 28

    Fact 28: CheckTag detaches parts on restart

    Game-specific#

    Vehicle parts and some held items (cassettes, radios, headlights) start in a CheckTag state that unparents the object and adds a Rigidbody. A restart therefore makes parts fall off cars and throws held items out of the hand; the log shows "Can't add component 'Rigidbody'".

    In practiceSet RestartOnEnable = false on CheckTag and LockPhysics while a mod holds the object.

    Related: 26 FSMs restart on re-enableSource notes: apocasaver-notes, apocapocket-rebuild-spec

  5. 29

    Fact 29: FSMs run while paused

    PlayMaker#

    PlayMaker FSMs keep running when Time.timeScale is 0, so button and mouse polls still fire in the pause menu. For example, clicking a menu button drops the held item.

    Source notes: PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS, apocasaver-notes

  6. 30

    Fact 30: Gameplay reads legacy input

    Game-specific#

    Gameplay FSMs poll legacy Input Manager buttons through PlayMaker GetButtonDown. The in-game Controls screen rebinds InsaneSystems InputController actions; the legacy axes keep their defaults.

    In practiceTo suppress a game action, prefix GetButtonDown.OnUpdate, filtered by FSM name and button name.

    Related: 31 Input button namesSource notes: apocapocket-rebuild-spec, healthstacks-notes

  7. 31

    Fact 31: Input button names

    Game-specific#

    Buttons read by the FSMs include Use, Weapon 1/2/3, Weapon off, Drop Weapon, Reload, Jump, Kick, Horn, Use Bandage, Use First Aid, Rotate Item, Change Camera and Adjust-*.

    Related: 30 Gameplay reads legacy inputSource notes: apocapocket-rebuild-spec, healthstacks-notes

  8. 32

    Fact 32: Hard-coded function keys

    Game-specific#

    F2 heals, F9 hides the UI and F11 toggles God mode. These keys are not listed in the Controls screen.

    In practiceDefault mod hotkeys to none or to a key verified to be free.

    Related: 33 God mode is noclip, not invulnerabilitySource notes: apocapocket-notes, apocaraiders-latest

  9. 33

    Fact 33: God mode is noclip, not invulnerability

    Game-specific#

    F11 God mode disables collision, gravity and movement FSMs and moves the player to layer 5 (UI). NPCs miss the player only because their sensors do not include that layer; Health is unchanged.

    Related: 32 Hard-coded function keysSource notes: apocaraiders-latest

  10. 34

    Fact 34: FSM lookup traps

    PlayMakerAdded#

    FsmVariables.GetFsmInt(name) and the other GetFsm* methods return a new placeholder variable when the name does not exist, so they cannot test for existence. PlayMakerFSM.FindFsmOnGameObject logs a warning for every miss.

    In practiceUse the Find* variable methods, iterate GetComponents<PlayMakerFSM>() matching FsmName, and check Fsm.Initialized before reading states.

    Source notes: apocaraiders-tracers, apocapocket-notes

    Revised 2026-10-13. Added.

Physics and collision queries 10 facts

Project physics settings and query limits that affect raycasts, overlaps and sweeps.

  1. 35

    Fact 35: Queries hit triggers and backfaces

    Game-specific#

    The project sets Physics.queriesHitTriggers = true and Physics.queriesHitBackfaces = true.

    Related: 36 Raycasts hit trigger collidersSource notes: apocaraiders-tracers

  2. 36

    Fact 36: Raycasts hit trigger colliders

    Game-specific#

    Because of that setting, raycasts hit trigger colliders, including the Player's and NPCs' head triggers.

    Related: 37 Head triggers double damageSource notes: apocaraiders-tracers

  3. 37

    Fact 37: Head triggers double damage

    Game-specific#

    Human NPCs carry a trigger capsule on mixamorig:Head with its own Bodypart FSM, which doubles the damage it receives.

    Related: 36 Raycasts hit trigger collidersSource notes: apocaraiders-tracers

  4. 38

    Fact 38: detectCollisions hides objects from raycasts

    Unity / .NET#

    Setting Rigidbody.detectCollisions = false also hides the object's colliders from raycasts.

    In practiceTo make an object non-solid but still hittable, set its colliders to isTrigger instead.

    Related: 39 detectCollisions is savedSource notes: apocapatrol-riders-2.1.0

  5. 39

    Fact 39: detectCollisions is saved

    Game-specific#

    ES3 saves detectCollisions with the object, so a value a mod leaves behind persists across loads.

    In practiceNever change detectCollisions on objects the game saves.

    Related: 38 detectCollisions hides objects from raycastsSource notes: apocasaver-notes

  6. 40

    Fact 40: ClosestPoint fails on non-convex meshes

    Unity / .NET#

    Collider.ClosestPoint returns the input point unchanged for non-convex MeshColliders. Only Box, Sphere, Capsule and convex Mesh colliders are supported.

    In practiceUse Physics.ComputePenetration or ClosestPointOnBounds instead.

    Related: 41 ClosestPoint fails on terrainSource notes: apocaraider-review-1.0.0, apocaraiders-latest

  7. 41

    Fact 41: ClosestPoint fails on terrain

    Unity / .NETCorrected#

    Collider.ClosestPoint also returns the input point unchanged for TerrainColliders.

    Related: 40 ClosestPoint fails on non-convex meshesSource notes: apocaraider-review-1.0.0

    Revised 2026-10-13. Corrected: observed behaviour, not a possibility.

  8. 42

    Fact 42: Overlap tests miss enclosed volumes

    Unity / .NETCorrected#

    Overlap tests such as CheckCapsule and OverlapCapsule against a non-convex MeshCollider detect only intersections with its triangles. A capsule wholly inside the rock reads as free.

    In practiceTo test whether a point is inside solid geometry, raycast upward with backface hits enabled; any hit means the point is enclosed.

    Related: 35 Queries hit triggers and backfacesSource notes: apocaraiders-tracers

    Revised 2026-10-13. Corrected: observed on non-convex MeshColliders only, not on terrain.

  9. 43

    Fact 43: One hit per collider

    Unity / .NETClarified#

    Physics.RaycastAll and RaycastNonAlloc return at most one hit per collider. A cave built from one mesh collider returns only its roof.

    In practiceTo find every surface along a ray, repeat single raycasts starting just past each hit.

    Source notes: apocalypterai-compat

    Revised 2026-10-13. Clarified: standard Unity behaviour, not specific to this game.

  10. 44

    Fact 44: Sweeps ignore starting overlaps

    Unity / .NETClarified#

    Sweep queries (SphereCast, CapsuleCast) ignore colliders that already overlap the shape at its starting position.

    In practiceRun an overlap test at the start position first to find what the body is already touching.

    Source notes: apocaraiders-tracers, warrig-notes

    Revised 2026-10-13. Clarified: standard Unity behaviour, not specific to this game.

NPCs, vehicles and hierarchies 7 facts

How NPCs move, and how the player and NPCs are parented while in vehicles.

  1. 45

    Fact 45: No NavMesh

    Game-specific#

    Vanilla NPCs do not use a baked Unity NavMesh, and the game ships none.

    Related: 46 Vanilla chase is a random walk, 47 AIModule is availableSource notes: enemy-car-research, apocaraiders-tracers

  2. 46

    Fact 46: Vanilla chase is a random walk

    Game-specific#

    NPCs chase by running forward while a Rotate FSM turns them randomly and bumper raycasts (layers 0, 8, 11 and 16, without Ground) steer them off obstacles. Idle NPCs stand still; nothing makes them wander.

    Related: 45 No NavMeshSource notes: apocaraiders-tracers, npcai-ai-export-humans-animals

  3. 47

    Fact 47: AIModule is available

    Game-specific#

    UnityEngine.AIModule.dll ships with the game, so a mod can bake a NavMesh at runtime.

    Related: 45 No NavMeshSource notes: apocaraiders-tracers

  4. 48

    Fact 48: The Player rides inside the car

    Game-specific#

    While driving, the Player object is parented at <car>/DriveTrigger/sitPos/Player.

    In practiceFind the Player by name, not by assuming it is a scene root.

    Related: 7 Distances are measured from the PlayerSource notes: apocapatrol-notes, apocasaver-notes

  5. 49

    Fact 49: Seated NPCs belong to the car

    Game-specific#

    NPCs seated in a vehicle are its descendants, so their transform.root is the car.

    Related: 50 Finding the NPC that owns a colliderSource notes: gunplay-notes

  6. 50

    Fact 50: Finding the NPC that owns a collider

    Game-specificCorrected#

    A creature root (human or animal) carries a Health FSM, and its body parts carry Bodypart FSMs that add damage to it. The Player also has a Health FSM, and corpses (<Name>_Dead) have Bodypart FSMs but no Health.

    In practiceWalk up from the hit collider to the first object with a Health FSM, and stop at a vehicle boundary.

    Related: 51 Recognising vehicle boundariesSource notes: gunplay-notes, apocaraiders-latest

    Revised 2026-10-13. Corrected: notes that the Player also has Health and corpses do not.

  7. 51

    Fact 51: Recognising vehicle boundaries

    Game-specificCorrected#

    Attached vehicle parts are tagged vehPart (layer 8); detached parts are tagged vehPartRemoved (layer 9). A vehicle root has an NWH VehicleController component.

    Related: 50 Finding the NPC that owns a colliderSource notes: gunplay-notes, apocapatrol-notes

    Revised 2026-10-13. Corrected: vehPart is a tag, not an object name.

Plugin runtime 5 facts

BepInEx and Unity behaviour every plugin for this game runs into.

  1. 52

    Fact 52: The game destroys the plugin's own GameObject

    Game-specificCorrected#

    On scene load the game destroys the BepInEx plugin's GameObject, even though BepInEx marks it DontDestroyOnLoad. As a result BaseUnityPlugin.Update, LateUpdate and OnGUI never run, and Chainloader.PluginInfos[guid].Instance reads as a destroyed object.

    In practiceRun per-frame logic on a separate hidden GameObject (HideAndDontSave plus DontDestroyOnLoad), recreate it on SceneManager.sceneLoaded, and re-find game references after every load. Test plugin presence with ReferenceEquals(info.Instance, null).

    Source notes: apocatemplater-notes, apocapocket-rebuild-spec, npcai-review-1.0.1, apocasetter-notes

    Revised 2026-10-13. Corrected: the trap is the plugin's own persistent object, not ordinary scene objects.

  2. 53

    Fact 53: Undo your own changes before the game takes over

    Game-specificCorrected#

    Vanilla release paths only restore what vanilla changed. When the game drops a weapon or releases an item, it re-enables what it disabled itself; colliders, gravity or renderers a mod switched off stay off, and the object can fall through the terrain.

    In practiceBefore handing an object back to a vanilla FSM, restore every change the mod made to it.

    Source notes: apocapocket-rebuild-spec, apocapocket-2.0-notes

    Revised 2026-10-13. Corrected: the earlier wording reversed who must clean up.

  3. 54

    Fact 54: Plugin versions must be plain numbers

    Game-specificAdded#

    BepInEx parses the [BepInPlugin] version as System.Version. A version like 1.2.0-beta makes it skip the plugin silently.

    In practiceUse plain versions such as 1.2.0. If a mod is missing from the load list, check its version first.

    Source notes: PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS, apocapocket-rebuild-spec

    Revised 2026-10-13. Added.

  4. 55

    Fact 55: Parse numbers with the invariant culture

    Unity / .NETAdded#

    Players run the game with comma-decimal locales. Float parsing or formatting with the current culture threw exceptions at startup for French, Russian, Polish and Portuguese players and misread values for German ones.

    In practiceUse CultureInfo.InvariantCulture for every number you parse or format yourself.

    Source notes: apocasetter-notes, apocapatrol-notes

    Revised 2026-10-13. Added.

  5. 56

    Fact 56: JsonUtility drops nested arrays

    Unity / .NETAdded#

    Unity JsonUtility in a BepInEx plugin silently drops arrays of nested custom [Serializable] classes; plain string[] survives.

    In practiceUse a small hand-written JSON serializer and check the written output.

    Source notes: apocatemplater-notes, apocapocket-2.0-notes

    Revised 2026-10-13. Added.
